# Storm or Shine 🌕☀️
An interactive Moon-base survival and space-weather learning game that teaches children about solar storms, radiation shielding, resource management and astronaut decision-making.
 
**Hide from the storm, or keep the base shining?**

Storm or Shine is an interactive educational game where players command a small Moon base near the lunar south pole for a 10-day mission. Players must balance limited resources, protect their crew from solar storms, and complete scientific objectives while learning about space weather and radiation.

## 🚀 Features

- **Mission Management:** Manage Power, Oxygen, Shielding and Crew Dose.
- **Resource Strategy:** Choose between running the oxygen generator, greenhouse, laboratory, shielding systems or recharging the base.
- **Solar Storm Events:** Encounter solar flares, coronal mass ejections (CMEs) and solar energetic particle (SEP) events.
- **Two Difficulty Levels:** Cadet and Pilot modes offer different challenges.
- **Interactive Moon Base:** Explore a visual cutaway of the lunar habitat and interact with its modules.
- **Science Learning Cards:** Explore Quick Points, Deep Dives, scientific illustrations and Myth vs Real explanations.
- **Space Weather Data:** Includes a NASA DONKI live-data connection and clearly labelled sample events.
- **Responsive Interface:** Designed for desktop and mobile screens.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. Choose your difficulty level.
2. Review your mission briefing and resource levels.
3. Select up to three actions per day.
4. Monitor the solar storm forecast.
5. Choose whether to keep working or shelter the crew.
6. Review the mission results and explore the science learning card.
7. Survive the 10-day mission while meeting your scientific objectives.

## 🧪 What You'll Learn

- Solar flares and their effects.
- Coronal Mass Ejections (CMEs).
- Solar Energetic Particles (SEPs).
- Radiation exposure and shielding.
- Resource management in space habitats.
- Communication delays and astronaut autonomy.

## 📡 Data and Scientific References

The project is designed to incorporate archived and live space-weather information from NASA's Database of Notifications, Knowledge, Information (DONKI).

Additional scientific references include:
- NASA DONKI
- NASA Open Science Data Repository (OSDR)
- NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center

Sample events are used in preview mode and are not presented as real historical observations.

## 🌍 Project Purpose

Storm or Shine was developed as an educational project for the **2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge**, under the challenge theme of building a Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer.

Its central idea is simple: space exploration involves difficult trade-offs. Safety, scientific discovery and essential supplies all compete for limited resources.
